Affordable depth k* against the median position of the correct candidate, for 13 pipelines from the break-even table (12 plotted). k* = (300 s - preparation - time to solve the correct candidate) / time to reject one wrong candidate. Blue circles: incremental solver, primary campaigns. For SPECK the time to reject is the mean over keys of (online - ranking) / candidates attempted, which includes the correct solve, so it is approximate, and the solve term is zero; SPECK positions are the observed attempt index of the correct candidate, from recovered keys only. Orange squares: a fresh solver process per candidate, N=32 study. Hollow markers: fewer than 10 completed wrong-candidate attempts; cut-off attempts are excluded from the mean, so k* is an upper bound. Labels give recovered / runs. Points on or above the dotted line k* = position + 1 can reach the correct candidate. Not plotted: N=32 SIMON, 1 word, matrices off (Kaplan-Meier median solve time not reached by the deadline, so k* is not estimable).
